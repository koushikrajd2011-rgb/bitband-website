What to do?

  1. Pick this round's theme (or anything you like in the free days).
  2. Code your art with p5.js, Python, Canvas or SVG. Aim for about 5 hours or less.
  3. Render it at 180 x 20 px for the front and back of the band.
  4. Make a PR on GitHub with a folder named after you: your code, front.png and back.png.
  5. Fill in the form after your PR is accepted.

FAQs

What is BitBand?

A YSWS run in Hack Club where you code artwork and we turn it into your own NFC wristband.

Which tools can I use?

Only code: p5.js, Python, Canvas or SVG. No Figma or Procreate.

How long does it run?

About 3 weeks. Every 6 days a new themed round starts. Leftover days are free: make anything.

How long should a project take?

Around 5 hours or less on average. This may change.

What are BitDrops?

Secret, limited-time events that pop up on this site at random. Complete one to earn a holographic sticker sheet or a sticker.
